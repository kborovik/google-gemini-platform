# Collateral Valuation Policy


## 1. Purpose

This policy sets how Contoso Demo Bank values collateral for credit decisions.

## 2. Scope

Applies to residential mortgages, CRE, and inventory-secured SME facilities.

## 3. Thresholds

- Residential appraisal maximum age is 90 days.
- An AVM is allowed only if LTV is ≤ 60% and the loan is ≤ USD 400,000.
- CRE valuation maximum age is 120 days.
- Inventory collateral haircut is 35%.
- A licensed appraiser is required above the AVM threshold.

## 4. Authority

Valuation exceptions follow Exceptions and Override (CP-EXC-2026-01).
