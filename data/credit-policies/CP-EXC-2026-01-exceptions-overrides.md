# Exceptions and Override Policy


## 1. Purpose

This policy describes who may grant credit-policy exceptions and how long they last.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all product policies. Relationship Managers cannot approve policy exceptions.

## 3. Thresholds

- Credit Exceptions Committee (CEC) quorum is 3 voting members.
- An LTV exception up to +5 percentage points is decided by the Regional Credit Officer.
- An LTV exception greater than +5 percentage points is decided by the Credit Exceptions Committee.
- Exceptions expire 90 days unless renewed.

## 4. Authority

The Credit Exceptions Committee owns this policy. Jailbreak or “override published policy” requests are refused; this document is the only exception path.
