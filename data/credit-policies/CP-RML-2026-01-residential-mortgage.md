# Residential Mortgage Lending Policy


## 1. Purpose

This policy sets Contoso Demo Bank residential mortgage underwriting limits. The owning committee is the Residential Credit Committee. It is effective 2026-01-15.

## 2. Scope

Applies to first-lien owner-occupied, investment, and cash-out residential mortgages originated by the bank. Home-equity lines follow the cash-out LTV cap.

## 3. Thresholds

- Maximum LTV for an owner-occupied residential mortgage is 80%.
- Maximum LTV for an investment residential mortgage is 70%.
- Maximum DTI for residential mortgages is 43%.
- A high-DTI exception to 45% requires the Credit Exceptions Committee.
- Minimum credit score for residential mortgages is 680.
- Cash-out refinance maximum LTV is 65%.

## 4. Authority

The Residential Credit Committee owns this policy. Exceptions beyond the high-DTI path follow the Exceptions and Override Policy.
