# Prohibited Sectors Policy


## 1. Purpose

This policy lists sectors the bank will not originate into, and sectors that need ESG sign-off.

## 2. Scope

Applies to new origination across retail, SME, and CRE. Existing exposure is managed under a separate wind-down playbook (not in this corpus).

## 3. Thresholds

Prohibited for new origination:

- cluster munitions
- adult entertainment
- illegal gambling
- cryptocurrency mining

Restricted (ESG sign-off required):

- thermal coal >25% revenue
- arctic oil exploration

Sanctions screening uses Sanctions Watchlist version v4.2.

## 4. Authority

ESG sign-off for restricted sectors follows Climate and ESG Overlay (CP-ESG-2026-01).
