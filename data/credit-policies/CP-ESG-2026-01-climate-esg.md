# Climate and ESG Overlay Policy


## 1. Purpose

This overlay tightens CRE and large-facility credit where physical or transition climate risk is material.

## 2. Scope

Applies in addition to Commercial Real Estate (CP-CRE-2026-01). Where this overlay is more specific, it controls.

## 3. Thresholds

- A physical climate risk score is required for CRE in FEMA flood zone A/V.
- High climate-risk CRE maximum LTV is 55%.
- Financed-emissions reporting is required for facilities ≥ USD 1,000,000.
- A transition-risk questionnaire is mandatory for energy, transport, and agriculture NAICS.

## 4. Authority

This overlay is more specific than the stabilized CRE LTV of 65% in CP-CRE-2026-01. Credit officers must quote both figures when they differ.
