# Unsecured Consumer Lending Policy


## 1. Purpose

This policy sets Contoso Demo Bank unsecured consumer (personal loan) limits.

## 2. Scope

Applies to unsecured personal loans to natural persons. Related-party unsecured consumer loans are prohibited under CP-RPL-2026-01.

## 3. Thresholds

- Maximum personal loan amount is USD 50,000.
- Maximum term is 60 months.
- Minimum FICO is 700.
- Maximum DTI for unsecured consumer loans is 36%.
- Rate floor is 8.75% APR.
- The bank originates no interest-only unsecured loans.

## 4. Authority

Relationship Manager unsecured approval limits are in Delegated Credit Authority (CP-AUTH-2026-01). Amounts above the personal-loan cap are out of policy.

See Related-Party Lending (CP-RPL-2026-01) and Delegated Credit Authority (CP-AUTH-2026-01).
