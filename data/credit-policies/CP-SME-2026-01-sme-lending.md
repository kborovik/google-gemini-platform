# SME Lending Policy


## 1. Purpose

This policy sets Contoso Demo Bank small and medium enterprise (SME) lending limits.

## 2. Scope

Applies to working-capital, term, and revolving facilities to SME borrowers. Construction SME facilities also follow CP-CND-2026-01.

## 3. Thresholds

- Maximum SME facility is USD 2,500,000.
- A personal guarantee is required above USD 250,000.
- Working-capital maximum tenor is 12 months.
- Sector concentration cap is 15% of the SME book per NAICS 2-digit code.
- Minimum years in operation is 3 years.

## 4. Authority

Regional Credit Officer SME limits are in Delegated Credit Authority (CP-AUTH-2026-01).
