# Delegated Credit Authority Policy


## 1. Purpose

This policy assigns approval limits by role. Amounts above a role’s limit go to the Credit Committee.

## 2. Scope

Applies to origination and limit increases. Policy exceptions are not delegated here; see CP-EXC-2026-01.

## 3. Thresholds

- Relationship Manager unsecured approval is ≤ USD 25,000.
- Branch Credit Manager residential approval is ≤ USD 750,000 and LTV ≤ 75%.
- Regional Credit Officer SME approval is ≤ USD 1,000,000.
- Above these limits → Credit Committee.

## 4. Authority

The Credit Committee owns this matrix. Relationship Managers cannot approve policy exceptions (CP-EXC-2026-01).
