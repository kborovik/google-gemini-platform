# Construction and Development Lending Policy


## 1. Purpose

This policy sets land, construction, and development lending limits.

## 2. Scope

Applies to land acquisition and construction facilities. Stabilized CRE after conversion follows CP-CRE-2026-01.

## 3. Thresholds

- Maximum LTV for land is 50%.
- Interest reserve minimum is 12 months.
- Inspection frequency is monthly.
- A completion guarantee is required.
- Maximum interest-only during construction is 18 months.

## 4. Authority

CRE construction LTV in CP-CRE-2026-01 (55%) is for construction of income-producing CRE; land LTV in this policy is 50%.
