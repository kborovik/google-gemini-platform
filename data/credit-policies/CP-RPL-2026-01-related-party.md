# Related-Party Lending Policy


## 1. Purpose

This policy limits lending to related parties of Contoso Demo Bank.

## 2. Scope

Applies to directors, officers, and owners at or above the ownership threshold, and to entities they control.

## 3. Thresholds

- A related party is ≥10% ownership or a board seat.
- Approval authority is the Board Credit Subcommittee.
- Maximum aggregate related-party exposure is 5% of Tier 1 capital.
- The bank originates no related-party unsecured consumer loans.

## 4. Authority

Only the Board Credit Subcommittee may approve related-party credit. Product committees cannot waive this policy.
