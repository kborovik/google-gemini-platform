# Credit Documentation Policy


## 1. Purpose

This policy sets minimum credit-file documents and retention.

## 2. Scope

Applies to consumer, residential, SME, and CRE origination files.

## 3. Thresholds

- Self-employed borrowers: 2 years tax returns plus YTD P&L.
- Wage earners: last 2 pay stubs and W-2.
- File retention is 7 years after payoff.
- KYC refresh every 36 months for existing SME customers.

## 4. Authority

Incomplete files may not be approved under Delegated Credit Authority (CP-AUTH-2026-01).
