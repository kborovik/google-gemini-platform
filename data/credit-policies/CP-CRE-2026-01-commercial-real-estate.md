# Commercial Real Estate Lending Policy


## 1. Purpose

This policy sets Contoso Demo Bank commercial real estate (CRE) underwriting limits. The owning committee is the Commercial Credit Committee.

## 2. Scope

Applies to stabilized income-producing CRE and CRE construction facilities. Land-only loans follow Construction and Development (CP-CND-2026-01). Climate overlays in CP-ESG-2026-01 may tighten LTV.

## 3. Thresholds

- Maximum LTV for stabilized CRE is 65%.
- Minimum DSCR for stabilized commercial real estate is 1.25x.
- Construction maximum LTV is 55%.
- Interest-only period on CRE may not exceed 24 months.
- Underwriting must apply a vacancy stress of 10%.

## 4. Authority

The Commercial Credit Committee owns this policy. Climate-risk LTV reductions in the ESG overlay are more specific than the stabilized CRE LTV in this document.
